A gift for someone in prison

People inside can't order books from me. Federal prisons only take books shipped straight from a publisher or a bookstore, so order it on Amazon and ship it to them at their facility. Put their full name and register number on the address.

It's already on its way to 26 women's federal prison libraries, with a note: For your library. Pass it on.

About Dawn

Dawn de Brantes (TheHippieDiva) did four and a half years in federal prison and came home to build a life from the dirt up: a homestead, puppies, music and this book.

Questions

Can I send a book to an inmate in federal prison?

Yes, if it ships straight from a publisher or bookstore (like Amazon). Every facility has its own mail rules, so check bop.gov for theirs.

Is it only for women?

It's written for women inside, but anyone cooking without a kitchen can use it: dorm rooms, hotel rooms, camping.
